# Pitch Deck: Sovereign-Middleware (RegTech)

## 1. The Friction
SME adoption of AI is stalling. Enterprises want to use K-Cloud models but are legally paralyzed by the 2026 AI Basic Act and PIPA 3% revenue-risk penalties. Current IT tools are diagnostic, not defensive.

## 2. The Solution
**Sovereign-Middleware:** A software-defined guardrail that sits as a transparent proxy. It provides real-time PII interception and cryptographic audit-trails for K-Cloud infrastructure.

## 3. Product Architecture
- **Guardrail Proxy:** Real-time request interception in Go.
- **Immutable Ledger:** SHA-256 chained audit logs.
- **Policy-as-Code:** YAML-driven compliance enforcement.
- **MSS Artifacts:** One-click compliance reporting for regulators.

## 4. Market Scalability
- **TAM:** $1.47B KR RegTech market.
- **GTM:** Compliance-led entry. SMEs install via Terraform in minutes, not months.
- **Unit Economics:** Subscription-based liability coverage vs. per-query pricing.

## 5. The KSGC Winner Narrative
- **Alignment:** Total adherence to National AI Strategy 2027.
- **Feasibility:** Air-gap ready binary, zero-egress architecture.
- **Impact:** Solves the SME adoption crisis for K-Cloud.
- **Scalability:** Terraform/Ansible native deployment.
